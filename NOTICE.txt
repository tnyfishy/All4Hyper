Google features (app/src/main/kotlin/.../google, app/src/main/java/.../google, companion/)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
These come from XGoogle (https://github.com/Leekin2/XGoogle, GPL-3.0, by Leekin), a fork of
Coogle 1.2 (https://github.com/zopulus/coogle, GPL-3.0, by zopulus). The hooks derive from
MiToverG422/ONextBox (GPL-3.0), commit 1ce6ad3559c9c6ba33f8d8179b1ede55198961e2.
Only the parts that apply to HyperOS and to the four features offered here were taken:
  - the hook compatibility layer (google/compat)
  - AssistantRuntime, HookLog, LspConfig (switches now read All4Hyper's own preferences)
  - ContextualSearchHooks, XiaoAiNavRedirectHooks, AssistantFeatureHooks (contextual search report only)
  - AssistantKeepAliveHooks, AssistantVoiceHooks, NativeAssistantSessionRecovery
  - GmsRegionRestrictionHooker, QuickShareTileHooks
  - LiveTranslateHooks and HookInstaller (Coogle 1.3.x)
  - the companion module, with one change: a switch for the power key (/data/adb/coogle_power_off)
    next to the existing one for the navigation handle; its version is 1.3.1-a4h1 (132).
GoogleSystemHooks and GoogleEntry are All4Hyper's own wiring around those hooks.
The companion bundles Google enrollment components that remain subject to their original licenses.

Security Center hooks (app/src/main/java/.../hooks)
---------------------------------------------------
The class and method names these hooks target, and what each call does, were found by reading
MeowPower by yuhj319 (https://github.com/Xposed-Modules-Repo/io.github.yuhj319.meowpower), which has no
licence. The code here is a fresh implementation, not a copy.
